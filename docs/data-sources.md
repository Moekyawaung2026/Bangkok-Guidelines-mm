# Data Sources & Attribution

All transport data in Bangkok Go MM is strictly sourced from verified official authorities in Thailand:

1. **Bangkok Mass Transit Authority (BMTA / ขสมก.)**:
   - Official bus route numbers, fares, and stop islands.
   - https://www.bmta.co.th

2. **Bangkok Mass Transit System (BTS Skytrain)**:
   - Sukhumvit Line, Silom Line, Gold Line stations, exit numbers, and operating schedules.
   - https://www.bts.co.th

3. **Mass Rapid Transit Authority of Thailand (MRTA / MRT)**:
   - Blue Line, Purple Line, Yellow Line, Pink Line timetables and station connections.
   - https://www.mrta.co.th

4. **State Railway of Thailand (SRT / รฟท.)**:
   - Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal, Hua Lamphong, Don Mueang Red Line commuter rail.
   - https://www.railway.co.th

5. **Department of Land Transport (DLT / กรมการขนส่งทางบก)**:
   - Official meter taxi base fare regulations, airport surcharge policies, and 1584 complaint system.
   - https://www.dlt.go.th

6. **Royal Thai Police & Tourist Police**:
   - 191 Emergency Police & 1155 Tourist Police multilingual dispatch.

7. **Embassy of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar in Bangkok**:
   - Labor section contact, citizen assistance, and location verification.
   - 110 North Sathorn Road, Bangkok. Tel: +66 2 233 2237.
